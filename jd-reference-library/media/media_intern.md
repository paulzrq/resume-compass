## Implications for Our Framework
- The news/editorial track (WaPo) puts a track-specific work sample directly into the application checklist — a portfolio isn't a bonus but a gate. proj and present should be scored jointly and weighted more heavily than for the social-media track; gap ① ("missing or broken portfolio link") should be treated as near-disqualifying here.
- The video/content-production track (The Athletic) doesn't ask for a traditional news-writing sample, instead requiring a combination of "editing tools + news sense + subject-matter interest" — the skill dimension should focus on editing software and narrative pacing, and the "portfolio" under present takes the form of video rather than articles.
- The brand social-media track (Carnival) skews clearly toward "generalist business": GPA, Office skills, SEO literacy, and multi-platform familiarity, with no major restriction, and the strongest emphasis on "data-driven decisions" — this aligns closely with bonus item ② ("independently runs a social account with growth data"), which is most concentrated in this sub-track.
- The broadcast/production track (ABC News) explicitly requires "a portfolio link for the application to be considered complete" and additionally requires self-arranged housing/transportation — binding present and proj together more tightly than the news or social-media tracks. This kind of hidden gate is also a source of gaps easily overlooked in student resumes, leading to drop-off after initial screening.
- Compared with PR (Edelman), which values generalist soft skills — organizational/communication ability and demonstrable leadership — the news, video-content, and broadcast tracks all make a concrete, verifiable work sample/edit/writing sample a mandatory part of the application, meaning proj/present should generally be weighted higher across content-media roles than for the PR track.
- The four content sub-tracks show a clear gradient in how much they demand "data literacy": brand social (SEO/growth data) > news/broadcast (audience targeting, multi-platform adaptation) > video-content production (barely mentions audience data, emphasizing narrative and editing skill instead) — gap ③'s deduction weight should be adjusted accordingly by sub-track.
