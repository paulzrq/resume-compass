## Implications for Our Framework
- The brand-management posting (Unilever) never mentions any specific marketing tool in its basic requirements, while the digital/growth-marketing posting (Hill House Home) names Meta, TikTok, and Google plus CAC/ROAS metrics directly in the job duties — bonus item ② ("proficient with data-analysis/ad-platform tools") carries far more real weight for growth marketing than for brand management, and the two should not share one skill rubric.
- Product marketing (IBM) is the only track to state A/B testing and Excel/Python/Tableau explicitly in the basic requirements, showing it emphasizes "strategy + data" more than brand or content marketing — gap ② ("lacks quantifiable growth/conversion data") should carry heavier deduction weight when evaluating product-marketing resumes.
- The content-marketing/social-media track (WBD)'s bonus items center on hard content-production skills (video editing, AVID experience) and cultural sensitivity, with no data-analysis tools mentioned at all — bonus item ③ ("content creation/social-media growth results") should be treated as a core bonus item here, and the skill dimension should shift focus from "ad-platform tools" to "content-production tools."
- Market research/consumer insights (P&G) is the only track to put "data-analysis background + an insight-related discipline like sociology/psychology/anthropology" directly in the entry gate rather than as a bonus — gap ③ ("doesn't demonstrate insight into the target user/market") should escalate from a deduction to a near-disqualifying core criterion for this sub-track.
- The two tracks that most explicitly reward "past internship/project experience" as a bonus (IBM product marketing, Hill House Home growth marketing) are also the two that most emphasize independent ownership — brand management, content marketing, and market research phrase "past experience" more loosely — bonus item ① ("independently ran a campaign with measurable results") should not be weighted equally across all marketing sub-tracks.
