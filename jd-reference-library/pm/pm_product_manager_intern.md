## Implications for Our Framework
- Technical PM (NVIDIA) and growth/consumer PM (TikTok, Snap) require fundamentally different things under the skill dimension: the former requires Git, cloud deployment, AI/ML technical projects, and open-source contributions as basic requirements rather than bonuses; the latter centers on SQL/Excel-type analysis tools and leans more on internship/work experience than technical depth.
- Large companies (Google) and mid-size/startups (Gusto) emphasize different things under edu: Google values major fit and "0-to-1" founder spirit — a potential-oriented approach; Gusto sets "2+ years full-time experience plus a specific graduation window" as a hard gate — a seniority-oriented approach.
- Growth PM (TikTok) lists SQL/data-tool proficiency as a basic requirement, while Google and Snap list "data-driven decision-making experience" only as a bonus — confirming bonus item ② should carry significantly more weight in the growth track than elsewhere.
- Consumer app PM (Snap) skews toward "seniority/credentials" (1+ years full-time plus an in-progress MBA) with no technical background required, contrasting sharply with technical PM (which barely asks about years of experience, focusing instead on technical project depth) — the same resume should be read differently under edu/exp depending on the sub-track.
- Both the startup/mid-size track (Gusto) and the consumer track at a large company's product line (Snap) set explicit hard requirements around on-site work arrangements, a level of logistical detail almost never seen in large-company/technical-PM postings — the present dimension should note whether candidates clearly explain internship timing and location fit when evaluating small-company/consumer-track applications.
